# Gülümse Dental Clinic — Frequently Asked Questions

> This is a demo knowledge base for a fictional business.

## What are your opening hours?
The clinic is open on weekdays from 09:00 to 19:00 and on Saturdays from 10:00 to 16:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays. For emergencies outside weekday opening hours you can reach our on-call dentist by phone.

## Where is the clinic and how do I get there?
The clinic is on Moda Street in Kadıköy, Istanbul, on the 2nd floor of the Örnek Building. It is a 10-minute walk from the Kadıköy ferry pier. There is free parking for 6 cars behind the building for our patients.

## How can I book an appointment?
You can book through the appointment form on our website, our WhatsApp line or by phone. First examination appointments are usually available within 2-3 working days. If you cannot make it, please let us know at least 24 hours in advance.

## Is the first examination free?
The first examination and oral check-up are free of charge. If needed, a digital panoramic X-ray is taken during the visit; the panoramic X-ray costs 450 TL. At the end of the visit you receive a written treatment plan and price quote.

## How much does teeth whitening cost?
In-office teeth whitening takes about 60 minutes in a single session and costs 6,500 TL. The take-home whitening tray kit costs 4,000 TL. We recommend a scale and polish before whitening.

## How long does a scale and polish take and what does it cost?
A professional cleaning (scale and polish) takes about 30-45 minutes and costs 1,500 TL. We recommend having it done at least once a year.

## Can you tell me about dental implants?
An implant replaces a missing tooth with a titanium screw placed in the jawbone. Treatment usually takes 3-6 months depending on bone condition. A single-tooth implant starts from 22,000 TL; the exact price is set after the examination and X-ray. Our implants come with a lifetime manufacturer warranty.

## Do you offer orthodontics and clear aligners?
Yes. We offer metal and ceramic braces as well as clear aligner treatment. Treatment takes between 6 and 24 months depending on the case. Orthodontic prices are set after a free orthodontic assessment.

## Do you treat halitosis?
Yes. Halitosis is usually caused by bacterial plaque on the tongue, gum disease or decayed teeth. At the examination we identify the cause and prepare a plan of professional cleaning, gum treatment and tongue-cleaning advice. The halitosis check is included in the free first examination.

## Do you treat children?
Yes, our pediatric dentist sees children aged 3 and over. Fluoride application for children costs 700 TL and fissure sealants cost 600 TL per tooth.

## What payment options do you accept? Can I pay in installments?
We accept cash, debit cards and all credit cards. We offer up to 6 interest-free installments with partner credit cards. Patients whose treatment plan exceeds 20,000 TL can pay in up to 12 installments.

## Do you work with insurance?
We are a private clinic and do not have an agreement with the Turkish social security institution (SGK). We work with some private health insurance companies; contact us with your policy details to check your coverage.

## What should I do if I have an urgent toothache?
For severe toothache, swelling or dental trauma we offer same-day emergency appointments. Outside opening hours you can call our on-call dentist line. If you have rapidly spreading facial swelling, fever or difficulty breathing, go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## Do your treatments come with a warranty?
Fillings have a 2-year clinic warranty and zirconia crowns and bridges have a 5-year warranty. To keep the warranty valid you need a check-up once a year.

## Which languages do you speak?
Our team speaks Turkish and English. We prepare treatment plans in English for international patients.
