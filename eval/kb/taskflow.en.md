# TaskFlow — Help Center

> A fictional project management SaaS, written for evaluation tests.

## What plans do you offer?
TaskFlow has three plans. Starter is free for up to 3 users and 2 projects. Team costs $9 per user per month (billed annually) or $12 per user per month (billed monthly) and includes unlimited projects. Business costs $19 per user per month (billed annually) and adds SSO, audit logs and priority support.

## Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of the Business plan. No credit card is required to start the trial. When the trial ends, the workspace moves to the Starter plan unless you choose a paid plan.

## Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Annual Business plans can also be paid by bank transfer against an invoice. We do not accept PayPal or cryptocurrency.

## How do I cancel my subscription?
Workspace owners can cancel anytime under Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Monthly plans stay active until the end of the current billing period. Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within the first 30 days; after 30 days no refunds are given.

## Do you offer discounts?
Registered non-profit organisations and schools get 50% off the Team and Business plans. Contact sales with proof of status to apply. We do not offer other discounts or coupon codes.

## Integrations
TaskFlow integrates natively with Slack, Google Drive, GitHub and Zapier. There is no native integration with Microsoft Teams or Jira; you can connect them through Zapier.

## API and rate limits
All paid plans include access to the REST API. The rate limit is 100 requests per minute per workspace on Team and 500 requests per minute on Business. The Starter plan has no API access. Webhooks are available on the Business plan only.

## Security and data location
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt). TaskFlow is GDPR compliant; we are not yet SOC 2 certified. Two-factor authentication is available on all plans.

## Storage limits
Starter includes 1 GB of file storage per workspace, Team 100 GB per workspace and Business 1 TB per workspace. The maximum size of a single file upload is 250 MB.

## Support
Starter users get support through the community forum. Team customers get email support with a response within 2 business days. Business customers get priority email support with a response within 4 business hours. We do not offer phone support.

## Mobile apps and offline use
TaskFlow has mobile apps for iOS and Android. Offline mode is available in the mobile apps only; the web app requires an internet connection.

## Data export and deleting an account
You can export all tasks and comments as CSV or JSON from Settings > Data. When a workspace is deleted, all data is permanently removed within 30 days and cannot be restored.
